The goal of the game is to collaborate with the other
players to defuse a bomb.

You can see a denotator on the top on the left side,
it shows the remaining lifes. For every wrong cut you
loose a life and when you do not have lifes anymore or
if you cut the red wire, the bomb explodes.

Every player has 12 or 13 wires on their rack.

Each wire has a value ranging from 1 to 12.
Each blue value appears exactly four times.

There are two out of three known yellow wires (2/3)
and one out of two known red wires (1/2) in the game.
You can see the possible values that are in the game on
the top ("Possible yellow and red wires").
All yellow wires end in ".1" and all red wired end in ".5".
Their exact values are unknown.

All wire holders are sorted in ascending order.

You have to click on two of your wires (same value or both yellow) to make a cut.

Only possible if the other two wires with the value you choose are cut already or if you have both yellow wires.

You have to click on four of your wires (same value) to make a cut.

Only possible you have all four wires of a value.

You have to choose a player and click on one of your wires and one of theirs to make a cut with their wire. The cut is successful, if your target wire has the same value as your wire or when they are both yellow.

You can cut the red wire if its the last wire on your rack.

Only possible if the red wire is the last wire, that's not cutted, on your rack.

You can use an equipment at any time for your cut.

For a successful cut, you need at least two blue wires with the same value or two yellow wires.
If your unsure, which wire to cut, you can choose equipment to make the cut more save.
