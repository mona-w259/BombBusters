Actions

You have to click on two of your wires (same value) to make a cut.

Only possible if the other two wires with the value you choose are cut already or if you have both yellow wires.

You have to click on four of your wires (same value) to make a cut.

You have to choose a player and click on one of your wires and one of theirs to make a cut with their wire.

You can cut the red wire if its the last wire on your rack.

You can use an equipment if you do not know what to cut.

Equipment

If you perform a duo cut, you may name 1 value (except yellow) and point to 2 cables belonging to 1 team member, instead of just one cable.

This is your personal equipment, only you can use it.

This equipment works like the double detector, but with 3 cables.
